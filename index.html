A Non-Linear Background

The Chemist Who Was Banned From CMU Computer Science

At Carnegie Mellon University, I earned my B.S. in Chemistry from the Mellon College of Science and spent over a year in Dr. Veronica Hinman’s Evolutionary & Developmental Biology Lab knocking down genes in sea stars and sea urchins to study regeneration. When I tried taking CMU Computer Science courses, I did so poorly that the university told me I couldn’t take any more. Naturally, I became a software engineer anyway—spending over two years building enterprise systems at Adobe before joining Google as an Android Software Engineer and later transitioning into Technical Program Management.

Calling Cues, Catching on the Trapeze & $1 Movies

Before leading 100-engineer software launches, I lived in the theatre booth and on the flying trapeze rig. I spent summers at Camp Cayuga working the board on the flying trapeze rig, catching flyers, and performing on lyra. On campus, I served as Production Stage Manager, Director, and Actor across 25+ shows (from Assassins and Bat Boy to directing the Rocky Horror shadow cast), acquired a $30,000 auditorium cinema projector as an AB Films projectionist, and chaired Greek Sing—producing 13-minute abridged musicals (Wicked, American Idiot) that raised over $200,000 for Pittsburgh charities.
